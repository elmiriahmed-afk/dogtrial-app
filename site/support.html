Questions, problems, or feedback about DogTrial

Account & sign-in

If you can't create an account or log in, double check your email is entered correctly and your password is at least 8 characters. If you're still stuck, email us the address you signed up with (never your password) and we'll help sort it out.

Something looks wrong in the app

DogTrial is under active development, and we fix reported issues quickly. When you email us about a bug, it helps to include: what screen you were on, what you tapped, and what you expected to happen instead.

Data & privacy questions

Most of your trial data (feeding, walks, training, journal) lives only on your device and never reaches our servers. For the full breakdown of what's stored and where, see our Privacy Policy.

Feature requests

Ideas for what DogTrial should simulate next are always welcome — send them to the same address above.

# DogTrial — product facts for marketing runs

Verified from the code and store consoles on 2026-10-08. Update this file when facts change; every agent run reads it.

## Product
- DogTrial: mobile app (iPhone + Android) and website (dogtrial.dog). The user lives 30 days with a simulated dog to learn what life with a dog really involves before deciding to adopt a real one.
- Features: daily care (meals, bathroom needs, walks tracked by GPS or step counter), 15 training lessons (14 with a short video), budget estimate, breed- and age-aware care reminders (local notifications), a final 30-day report.
- 10 breeds: Labrador, Husky, French Bulldog, Border Collie, Chihuahua, American Staffordshire Terrier, Cane Corso, Belgian Malinois, German Shepherd, Bichon.
- Languages: English and French.
- It is a decision aid, not veterinary advice and not a guarantee of a successful adoption.

## Availability (changes — confirm with the user before claiming anything)
- iOS: approved by Apple; on sale in 148 countries/regions outside the EU (changes show in the App Store within 24 h). The 27 EU countries, including France, are blocked until the EU trader-status declaration is made in App Store Connect.
- Android: closed test running; request for Google Play production access sent on 2026-10-07, answer expected within about 7 days.
